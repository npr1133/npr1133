Let's explore the city of Changhua

Welcome to Changhua

Changhua City is a historic and culturally municipality in central Taiwan, proudly known as one of the island's earliest settled regions. Snuggled right between the rolling plains and the scenic Bagua Plateau, the city is instantly recognised by the iconic Baguashan Great Buddha, a massive 23-metre-tall concrete statue that looks out over the urban landscape from its hilltop perch. Railway enthusiasts travel from all over the world to see the city's famous Changhua Fan-Shaped Roundhouse, an active 1922 train depot known affectionately as the "hotel for trains" and the last surviving operational structure of its kind in Taiwan.

Lukang longshan temple

Lukang Longshan Temple is a national historic landmark in Changhua County and stands as Taiwan's most magnificent, best-preserved masterpiece of Qing Dynasty architecture.

Changhua roundhouse

The Changhua Fan-Shaped Roundhouse is a globally rare, living railway heritage site built in 1922 and located just north of Changhua Train Station. It is the very last surviving operational roundhouse in Taiwan and one of only a few still working anywhere in the world.
