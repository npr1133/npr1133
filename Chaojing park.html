Get to know more about Chaojing Park

Chaojing Park is a remarkably revitalised coastal park on the eastern side of the Badouzi Peninsula in Keelung City, Taiwan. Once a massive coastal landfill site, it was completely transformed by the National Museum of Marine Science and Technology (NMMST) into an eco-friendly park renowned for its sweeping Pacific Ocean panoramas, vibrant intertidal ecology, and quirky artistic flair. The park's name, "Chaojing," refers to the marine zone where warm and cold ocean currents converge, creating an incredibly rich hotspot for ocean life
