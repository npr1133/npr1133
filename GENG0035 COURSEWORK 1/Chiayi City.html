Let's explore the city of Chiayi

Welcome to Chiayi

Chiayi City is a charming and historically rich municipality located on the fertile Chianan Plain in southwestern Taiwan. Globally celebrated as the historical gateway to the mist-shrouded peaks of the Alishan National Scenic Area, the city serves as the official starting point for the world-famous Alishan Forest Railway.

Hinoki Village

Hinoki Village is a beautiful outdoor village in Chiayi City made of traditional Japanese wooden houses. These buildings were originally built a long time ago out of cypress wood for workers in the local logging industry. Today, the village has been carefully restored and is filled with cute boutique shops, cosy cafés, and art studios for visitors to explore.

Alishan Forest Railway Garage Park

The Alishan Forest Railway Garage Park is an open-air railway museum and active train depot located in the East District of Chiayi City. Serving as the primary maintenance base camp for the Alishan Forest Railway, the leafy park showcases a treasure trove of retired narrow-gauge rolling stock.
