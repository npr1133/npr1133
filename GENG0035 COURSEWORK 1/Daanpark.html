Get to know the central park of Taipei City

Daan Forest Park is a massive 26-hectare public park located right in the heart of Taipei’s busy Da'an District. Opened in 1994, it is officially known as the "lungs of Taipei" because its thousands of trees and wide lawns provide a vital ecological shield and a refreshing escape from the concrete city. Often compared to Central Park in New York, it serves as a green sanctuary for both city residents and local wildlife.
