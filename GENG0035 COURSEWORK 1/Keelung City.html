Let's explore the city of Keelung

Welcome to Keelung

Keelung City is a captivating port city nestled among lush mountains in northern Taiwan, located just a short train ride from Taipei. Nicknamed the "Rainy Port" due to its frequent maritime showers, it serves as the island’s second-largest seaport and a major gateway for international cruise ships. The city is internationally celebrated for the Miaokou Night Market, a legendary culinary hub packed with vibrant yellow lanterns and exceptional seafood street stalls. It also boasts a striking mix of history and modern charm, featuring rugged coastal formations at Heping Island Geopark, historic military forts, and the brightly painted waterfront houses of Zhengbin Fishing Port.

Heping island geopark

Heping Island Geopark is a stunning coastal park located on Heping Island in the Zhongzheng District of Keelung City. Over millions of years, constant wave erosion and strong northeast winds have sculpted the rugged shoreline into a surreal gallery of unique geological formations, including mushroom rocks, tofu slabs, and sea caves. Beyond its remarkable geology and scenic walking trails, the park features a popular natural seawater swimming pool filled with marine life, offering visitors a safe place to swim directly alongside the ocean.

Chaojing park

Chaojing Park is a beautifully transformed seaside park located on the east side of the Badouzi Peninsula in Keelung City. Once a prominent coastal landfill, the area was revitalised by the National Museum of Marine Science and Technology into a sprawling green oasis complete with broad meadows, cycling paths, and striking large-scale outdoor art installations. It is highly popular for its wide-open lawns perfect for flying kites, its rich intertidal marine life, and its sweeping ocean views that stretch across the bay toward the glowing hillside town of Jiufen.
