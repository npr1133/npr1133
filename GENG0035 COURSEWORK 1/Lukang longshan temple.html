Get to know more about Lukang Longshan Temple

Lukang Longshan Temple (鹿港龍山寺) is a premier national historic site located in Lukang Township, Changhua County, widely regarded as the finest and most complete example of Qing Dynasty Buddhist architecture in Taiwan. Originally founded in the late Ming Dynasty and relocated to its current site in 1786, this spacious 5,000-square-meter complex is dedicated primarily to Guanyin, the Goddess of Mercy. Often affectionately nicknamed the "Forbidden City of Taiwan," it is deeply revered for its grand layout, preservation of ancient building techniques, and exquisite craftsmanship that avoids the flashy, modern neon updates seen at other temples
