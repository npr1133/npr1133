Get to know more about Nanliao fishing harbor

Nanliao Fishing Harbor, also known as Hsinchu Fish Harbor, is a vibrant working port located in the North District of Hsinchu City, Taiwan. Originally established as a distribution center for local fishermen, the harbor has successfully transformed into a popular recreational hub combining marine commerce with coastal tourism. Visitors flock to its famous two-story seafood market, where they can purchase fresh daily catches on the ground level and have them custom-cooked at upper-floor eateries. The port also serves as the scenic starting point for the renowned 17-kilometer Coastal Path, a favorite trail for scenic cycling and enjoying coastal views. With its vast, windy open spaces, the harbor grounds double as a world-class destination for flying kites and watching spectacular ocean sunsets
