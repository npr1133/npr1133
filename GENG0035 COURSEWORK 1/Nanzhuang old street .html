Get to know more about Nanzhuang old street

Nanzhuang Old Street (南庄老街), primarily centered along Zhongzheng Road and the highly famous Osmanthus Lane (桂花巷) in Miaoli County, is one of Taiwan's most celebrated historic mountain settlements. Originally booming during the Japanese colonial era due to the region's thriving camphor logging and coal mining industries, this narrow labyrinth of brick-lined alleys has gracefully transitioned into a premier cultural tourism destination
