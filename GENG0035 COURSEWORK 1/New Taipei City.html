Let's explore the city of New Taipei

Welcome to New Taipei

New Taipei City is a vibrant metropolis in northern Taiwan that completely encircles their capital city, Taipei. As the island's most populous city, it seamlessly blends bustling urban districts with breathtaking natural landscapes, ranging from the rugged northeast coastline to the lush, mountainous interior. The city is a major economic hub, hosting a diverse array of high-tech industries, traditional manufacturing zones, and bustling commercial centres. It is also celebrated for its rich cultural heritage, featuring historic destinations like the old mining town of Jiufen and the historic streets of Tamsui. An efficient public transport network, including extensive metro lines and high-speed rail links, The Taipei Metro, keeps the city closely integrated with the wider Taipei metropolitan area.

Shifen waterfall

Shifen Waterfall is a beautiful and famous waterfall in New Taipei City. It is the widest waterfall in Taiwan, measuring about 20 metres high and 40 metres across. Because of its big, curved shape and powerful water, people often call it the "Little Niagara of Taiwan".

Yehliu geopark

Yehliu Geopark is a famous geological park located on a narrow cape in the Wanli District of New Taipei City. The 1,700-metre promontory is world-renowned for its surreal, Martian-like landscape shaped by millions of years of sea erosion and weathering. Visitors flock here to see uniquely shaped rock formations, most notably the iconic "Queen's Head" which resembles the elegant profile of a monarch.
