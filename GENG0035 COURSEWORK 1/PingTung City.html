Let's explore the city of Pingtung

Welcome to Pingtung

Pingtung City is the lively capital of Pingtung County in southern Taiwan. It is famous for its warm, tropical climate and rich agriculture, producing delicious fruits like wax apples. The city blends modern life with deep history, featuring old military villages that have been turned into trendy cultural spots.

Kenting National Park

Known for its vibrant fusion of dramatic coral coastlines, thick jungle canopies, and high-energy night markets, it is Taiwan’s ultimate coastal getaway.

Shengli Star Village

Shengli Star Village Creative Life Park (Victory Star V.I.P. Zone) is a beautifully restored cultural hub in Pingtung City. Originally built as a Japanese military officers' dormitory complex between 1928 and 1936, the 5.9-hectare area has been transformed into a charming, slow-paced village of historic red-brick walls and traditional wooden architecture.
