Get to know more about Shengli Star Village

Shengli Star Village Creative Life Park(勝利星村創意生活園區), located in Pingtung City, is Taiwan's largest and most completely preserved historic military village (juancun). Originally constructed in the 1920s and 1930s as residential dormitories for Japanese military officers, the area later housed high-ranking Kuomintang commanders after World War II. Today, the village features dozens of meticulously restored historical structures, including the prominent white-walled General's House that now serves as the park's primary visitor center. The entire historic district has been dynamically transformed into a vibrant cultural hub filled with independent bookstores, local craft boutiques, themed cafes, and artistic exhibition spaces. Conveniently situated just a 15-minute walk from Pingtung Railway Station, the park is completely free to enter, making it a highly accessible and popular day-trip destination.
