Let's explore the city of Taoyuan

Welcome to Taoyuan

Taoyuan City is a major industrial and technological metropolis in northwestern Taiwan, most famous for hosting the island's primary gateway, Taoyuan International Airport. As a rapidly growing city right next to Taipei, it blends a diverse modern economy with beautiful natural spaces, earning it the traditional nickname "The Kingdom of Thousand Ponds" due to its historic network of irrigation basins. The city features a vibrant mix of cultures—including significant Hakka, indigenous, and Southeast Asian migrant communities—which is wonderfully reflected in its lively culinary and night market scenes. Visitors can explore a wide variety of attractions, ranging from the high-tech shopping outlets in Qingpu to the serene mountain trails, old streets, and famous dried tofu shops in the scenic Daxi District.

Xpark aquarium

Xpark Aquarium is a state-of-the-art, urban public aquarium located in the Zhongli District of Taoyuan City, right next to the Taoyuan High-Speed Rail (THSR) station. Developed by the famous Japanese marine amusement park operator Yokohama Hakkeijima, it is Taiwan's first multi-sensory metropolitan aquatic park.

Gloria outlets

Gloria Outlets is Taiwan's premier open-air, American-style shopping village located in the Qingpu area of Taoyuan City. Spanning a massive multi-level outdoor complex, it houses more than 285 international luxury boutiques, designer fashion labels, and popular sportswear flagship stores offering substantial daily discounts.
