Get to know more about Dome of Light

The Dome of Light in Kaohsiung, Taiwan, is the world's largest public art installation made of individual pieces of colored glass. Created by renowned Italian artist Narcissus Quagliata, the masterpiece is built into the ceiling of the Formosa Boulevard MRT station. The vibrant dome spans 30 meters in diameter and is constructed from 4,500 individual glass panels. Through four distinct themes—Water, Earth, Light, and Fire—the artwork narrates the story of human life, growth, destruction, and rebirth. It serves as a major cultural landmark that transforms a daily transit hub into a breathtaking, immersive gallery for commuters and tourists alike.
