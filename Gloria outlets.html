Get to know the central park of Gloria Outlets

Gloria Outlets is Taiwan's very first open-air, American-style luxury outlet mall. Located in the Zhongli District of Taoyuan City, this sprawling retail destination is designed like a suburban shopping village with wide, pedestrian-friendly avenues, central fountains, and highly curated storefronts. Hosting over 250 international brands, the mall is universally famous for offering steep year-round discounts on past-season luxury goods, designer fashion, and sportswear. Because of its strategic location directly adjacent to major transit hubs and its close proximity to the Taoyuan International Airport, it has become the ultimate final stop for travelers to enjoy premium duty-free shopping before flying out of Taiwan
