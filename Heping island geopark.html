Get to know more about Heping Island Geopark

The Heping Island Geopark is a stunning coastal reserve located in the Zhongzheng District of Keelung City, Taiwan. Connected to the mainland by Taiwan's first sea bridge, this vibrant destination is famed for its spectacular geological formations sculpted over 20 million years by relentless wind and wave erosion. Beyond its natural beauty, the island holds massive historical weight; it was a 17th-century stronghold where Spanish colonists built Fort San Salvador, triggering historic conflicts between Spanish and Dutch forces. Today, it seamlessly balances a rugged geological classroom with a modern, eco-friendly seaside park
