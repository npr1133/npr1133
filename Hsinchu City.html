Let's explore the city of Hsinchu

Welcome to Hsinchu

Hsinchu City is a vibrant metropolis in northwestern Taiwan, globally renowned as the island's primary high-tech hub. Nicknamed the "Windy City" due to its brisk coastal climate, it hosts the prestigious Hsinchu Science Park, which serves as the birthplace and operational base for some of the world's leading semiconductor and electronic giants. Beyond its ultra-modern technology sector, the city possesses a deep cultural heritage, beautifully landmarks like the 19th-century Yin Hsi East Gate and the bustling Hsinchu City God Temple, which is framed by the legendary street food stalls serving traditional pork balls and rice noodles. The city also features extensive green escapes, ranging from the historic Hsinchu Zoo to the scenic coastal cycling paths along the Seventeen Kilometre Coastline.

Xiangshan wetland

Xiangshan Wetlands is a massive, ecologically rich coastal reserve spanning over 1,600 hectares along the southern coast of Hsinchu City. As the largest coastal wetland in northern Taiwan, its vast intertidal mudflats serve as a vital sanctuary for migratory birds and host an incredible abundance of wildlife, including mudskippers and over 50 species of fiddler crabs.

Nanliao fishing harbor

Nanliao Fishing Port is a maritime leisure hub located in the North District of Hsinchu City. Serving as the official starting point for the famous 17-kilometre Coastal Scenic Area, the harbor features grassy lawns that take full advantage of Hsinchu’s legendary windy climate, making it an island-wide favourite for flying kites.
