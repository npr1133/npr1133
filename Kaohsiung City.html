Let's explore the city of Kaohsiung

Welcome to Kaohsiung

Kaohsiung City is a sunny, bustling maritime metropolis located in southern Taiwan and serves as the island's largest seaport. Over the years, it has dynamically transformed from a heavy industrial centre into a thriving capital of culture and arts, famously highlighted by the creative warehouses of the Pier-2 Art Center. The city beautifully showcases a blend of urban landscapes and scenic waterscapes, featuring iconic landmarks like the peaceful Love River, the historic Cijin island, and the colourful Dragon and Tiger Pagodas at Lotus Pond.

Dome of light

The Dome of Light is a spectacular public artwork located inside Formosa Boulevard MRT Station in Kaohsiung City, Taiwan. Designed by renowned Italian artist Narcissus Quagliata, it stands as the world's largest public art installation made from individual pieces of coloured glass. Spanning a massive 30 metres in diameter, the dome features over 4,500 vibrant glass panels that dynamically illustrate the story of human life through the elements of water, earth, light, and fire.

Spring and autumn pavilions

The Spring and Autumn Pavilions are a stunning Taoist shrine complex built directly over the scenic Lotus Pond in Kaohsiung, Taiwan. Erected in 1953, these twin four-storey octagonal towers are dedicated to Kuan Kung, the God of War, and are connected by a picturesque bridge. A massive, vibrant statue of the Goddess of Mercy riding a dragon sits at the front, allowing visitors to walk inside the dragon's body to view colorful traditional paintings.
