Let's explore the city of Miaoli

Welcome to Miaoli

Miaoli County is Taiwan’s heartland of slow travel, famed for its breathtaking mountainous landscapes and rich Hakka cultural heritage. Located in north-central Taiwan between Hsinchu and Taichung, it is affectionately known as the "Mountain Town" due to its rugged peaks, deep valleys, and mist-covered forests. Rather than bustling city centers, Miaoli is heavily celebrated for its slow-living towns, artisanal crafts, and agricultural beauty.

Nanzhuang old street

Nanzhuang Old Street is a charming, nostalgic mountain village in Miaoli County, highly celebrated for its rich Hakka heritage and narrow, winding alleyways. Originally booming during the Japanese colonial era due to camphor and coal mining, the town has beautifully repurposed its mid-century history into one of northern Taiwan's most distinctive slow-travel culinary and cultural destinations.

Tai'an hot springs

Tai'an Hot Spring is a serene, hidden valley retreat tucked deep into the mountains of Miaoli County. First discovered by local Indigenous people in 1908 and later developed as a health sanctuary during the Japanese colonial era, it is widely regarded as one of Taiwan’s premier hot spring destinations.
