Get to know more about Shifen waterfall

The Shifen Waterfall, often hailed as the "Little Niagara of Taiwan," is a breathtaking curtain-style cascade located in the scenic Pingxi District of New Taipei City. Plunging 20 meters into a deep, emerald-green pool below, it stands as the widest waterfall in Taiwan with a span of approximately 40 meters. The powerful rush of water creates a constant, fine mist in the air, which frequently refracts the sunlight to paint vibrant rainbows across the canyon. Visitors can easily admire this spectacular natural wonder from multiple angles thanks to a well-maintained network of viewing platforms and walking trails. Surrounded by a lush, verdant forest, the waterfall offers a serene yet powerful escape that perfectly complements a visit to the nearby historic Shifen Old Street.
