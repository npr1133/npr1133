Let's explore the city of Taipei

Welcome to Taipei

Taipei is a vibrant metropolis where rich historical traditions blend seamlessly with modern innovation. From the iconic heights of Taipei 101 to the bustling, aroma-filled lanes of Shilin Night Market, the city offers an unforgettable mix of stunning architecture, world-class street food, and serene natural hot springs nearby. It is the capital and a special municipalities of Taiwan. It serves as the economic centre, political centre of Taiwan. Its location is surrounded by the New Taipei City, the largest city of Taiwan with both cities connected with Taipei Metro, the busiest MRT Metro in Taiwan. It's one of the most convenient transport system of Taipei and New Taipei City to access to many tourist attractions, historical building and government departments of Taiwan.

Taipei 101

A 508-meter skyscraper. Take the lift to the observatory for breathtaking area to enjoy the 360-degree views of Taipei and New Taipei. It also provides Taiwanese local foods inside the tower.

Da'an Forest Park

Often called the "Central Park of Taipei," this massive ecological park features forests, quiet ponds, and shaded walking paths. It also provides high quality of public washrooms and benches to allow people to rest.
