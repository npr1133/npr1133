Let's explore the city of Taitung

Welcome to Taitung

Taitung City is a peaceful coastal municipality in southeastern Taiwan, celebrated as the ultimate gateway to the island's pristine, slow-paced Pacific coastline. Tucked tightly between the dramatic peaks of the Central Mountain Range and the open ocean, the city beautifully preserves a rich cultural tapestry shaped by a large population of diverse indigenous communities, including the Amis, Puyuma, and Rukai peoples.

Sanxiantai

Sanxiantai is an iconic coastal nature reserve and island group located in the northern part of Chenggong Township, Taitung County. The area is world-famous for its striking, wave-shaped eight-arch footbridge that resembles a prostrate sea dragon stretching across the water to connect the mainland to a rocky volcanic islet.

Taitung forest park & flowing lake

Taitung Forest Park is a massive 280-hectare coastal wetland and urban oasis located at the mouth of the Beinan River in Taitung City. Nicknamed the "Black Forest" due to its immense, dark canopy of whistling pine windbreaks planted in the 1960s, the park features a network of scenic cycling paths connecting open meadows with three distinct lakes.
