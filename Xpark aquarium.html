Get to know the central park of Xpark aquarium

Xpark Aquarium is Taiwan’s first state-of-the-art metropolitan aquarium, located in the Zhongli District of Taoyuan City. Jointly developed with Yokohama Hakkeijima Sea Paradise from Japan, this massive three-story indoor facility steps away from traditional displays by using advanced digital mapping, sensory lighting, tailored acoustics, and even specific scents to create a fully immersive 360-degree ecosystem. Conveniently positioned right next to the Taoyuan HSR (High-Speed Rail) station and Glory Outlets, it functions as a highly accessible, premium edutainment center that brings the world's diverse marine environments right into the city.
