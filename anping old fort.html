Get to know the Anping old fort

The Anping Old Fort—originally named Fort Zeelandia—is the absolute cradle of modern Taiwanese history. Located in the historic Anping District of Tainan City, this monumental site was established in 1624 by the Dutch East India Company as their primary international trading hub and military headquarters in East Asia. In 1662, the legendary Ming loyalist Koxinga (Zheng Chenggong) besieged and captured the fortress, expelling the Dutch and transforming it into his royal residence, which firmly cemented the site's status as a symbol of Taiwanese heritage
