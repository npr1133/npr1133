Get to know the central park of Anping Tree House

The Anping Tree House is a fascinating example of nature completely reclaiming man-made architecture. Located just a short walk north of the Anping Old Fort, this site was originally a brick warehouse built in 1867 by the British trading firm Tait & Co. to store granulated sugar and camphor. After World War II and the eventual decline of the local salt industry, the warehouse was completely abandoned for decades. During this period, the surrounding wild banyan trees grew unchecked, sending aerial roots down the brick walls and taking over the roofline, turning the structures into a living, structural forest.
