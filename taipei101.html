Get to know more about Taiwan famous landmark

Taipei 101 is a famous 101-storey skyscraper in Taiwan that stands 508 metres tall. Its unique design looks like a giant stalk of bamboo, which symbolises growth and safety. Because Taiwan gets earthquakes and typhoons, the tower has a massive 660-tonne steel ball hanging inside the top floors that sways to keep the building steady during storms. Inside, visitors can ride super-fast lifts to the top observation decks for amazing city views, or explore a luxury shopping centre and restaurants at the bottom.
