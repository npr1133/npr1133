Get to know more about Yehliu Geopark

Yehliu Geopark is an otherworldly coastal cape stretching 1.7 kilometres into the sea along Taiwan's dramatic North Coast. Shaped by millions of years of wave erosion, weathering, and crustal movements, the landscape is famous for its unique sandstone structures that resemble something out of a science fiction film. The park’s crown jewel is the iconic Queen's Head, a natural mushroom rock that bears an uncanny resemblance to a royal profile. Visitors can stroll along well-marked pathways to discover other fascinating shapes, such as the Sea Candles, Fairy's Shoe, and Tofu Rocks. With its stunning ocean vistas and rare geological wonders, this park offers a striking, open-air educational adventure that highlights the raw creative power of nature
